## Additional Medicare Tax (0.9%)
| Filing Status | Threshold |
|--------------|-----------|
| Single | $200,000 |
| MFJ | $250,000 |
| MFS | $125,000 |

> **Important:** Additional Medicare Tax is NOT deductible. The 50% SE deduction applies only to regular SE tax.

## SE Tax Calculation
1. SE Tax Base = Net SE Income x 0.9235
2. Social Security = min(SE Tax Base, $184,500) x 12.4%
3. Medicare = SE Tax Base x 2.9%
4. Regular SE Tax = SS + Medicare
5. Additional Medicare = max(0, SE Tax Base - threshold) x 0.9%
6. **SE Deduction = Regular SE Tax x 50%** (excludes Additional Medicare)

# Key Thresholds

## Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT)
- **Rate:** 3.8%
- **Single:** $200,000
- **MFJ:** $250,000
- **MFS:** $125,000

Applies to: Interest, dividends, capital gains, rental income, royalties, passive income

Does NOT apply to: Wages, SE income, active business income, retirement distributions

## Qualified Business Income Deduction (QBID)
- **Deduction Rate:** 20% of QBI

### Thresholds
| Filing Status | Full Deduction Below | Phaseout Complete |
|--------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Single | $201,750 | $276,750 |
| MFJ | $403,500 | $553,500 |

### Above Threshold Limits
- **Option 1:** 50% of W-2 wages
- **Option 2:** 25% of W-2 wages + 2.5% of UBIA

### SSTB (No deduction above threshold)
Health, law, accounting, actuarial, performing arts, consulting, athletics, financial services, brokerage, investment management, trading

### Optimal W-2 Ratio: 28.57% (2/7)
Where 50% of W-2 wages = 20% of QBI

## SALT Cap
- **Limit:** $40,400 ($20,200 MFS)
- **Floor:** $10,000 ($5,000 MFS)
- **Phasedown begins:** $500,500 MAGI
- **Expiration:** December 31, 2029

## AMT
| Filing Status | Exemption | Phaseout Begins |
|--------------|-----------|-----------------|
| Single | $90,100 | $500,000 |
| MFJ | $140,200 | $1,000,000 |
